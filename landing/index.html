Bindery

Merge your audiobook chapter files into a single M4B with chapters, metadata, and cover art.

Download macOS · Windows · Linux

Features

Lossless merging

AAC chapter files are combined without any re-encoding or compression. Your audio stays bit-perfect.

Compression options

Choose from a wide range of bitrate and channel settings to reduce file size when you need a smaller audiobook.

Metadata editing

Add album artwork, author, narrator, and other details. Chapter markers are generated automatically from filenames.

Fast and intuitive

Drag and drop files or entire folders. Bindery merges them in record time using multi-core parallel processing.

How it works

1

Drag and drop

Drop your chapter files or an entire folder onto Bindery.

2

Review

Reorder chapters, edit metadata, and adjust output settings.

3

Bind

One click. Out comes a single M4B with chapters and artwork.
