Features

Built for audiobooks.

Bindery picks the fastest path. Remux when it can. Transcode only when it must.

Zero setup

ffmpeg bundled in. Download, open, bind. Nothing else to install.

Lossless remux

AAC files join directly, with no re-encoding. Near-instant. Bit-perfect.

Parallel transcoding

MP3s convert to AAC across every CPU core. Hours of audio in minutes.

Chapters & metadata

Title, author, cover art — auto-detected, fully editable. Chapters made from filenames.

Drag & drop

Drop files or a folder. Drag to reorder. Bitrate from 64 to 320 kbps.

Offline & private

No uploads. No accounts. No tracking. Your files stay on your machine.

FAQ

Common questions.

Which file formats does Bindery accept?
MP3, M4A and M4B inputs. Output is always a single M4B with chapter markers, cover art and metadata.
Does Bindery work offline?
Yes. Everything runs locally. No account, no uploads, no network calls during binding.
Are Intel Macs supported?
The macOS build is universal — Apple Silicon and Intel are both covered.
Does it handle DRM-protected audiobooks?
No. Bindery only processes unprotected audio files that you own.
How are chapters detected?
Chapter titles come from filenames by default. You can edit names, order and durations in the review step before binding.
Where does the output file go?
You pick the destination when you bind. Bindery never touches the source files.
