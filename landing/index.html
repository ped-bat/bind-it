Bindery

Chapters in. Book out.

Merge your audiobook chapter files into a single M4B with chapters, metadata, and cover art. Fast, lossless, and focused.

Download for macOS Apple Silicon · macOS 13+

Built for the job

Lossless remux

AAC chapter files are merged without re-encoding. Your audio stays bit-perfect.

Chapter metadata

Chapter markers are auto-generated from filenames. Edit titles, add cover art, set author info.

Parallel transcoding

Non-AAC files are transcoded across all CPU cores. Large books finish fast.

Drag and drop

Drop files or entire folders. Bindery sorts them and gets to work.

How it works

1

Drop files

Drag your chapter files or a folder onto Bindery.

2

Edit metadata

Review chapters, set the title, author, and cover art.

3

Bind

One click. Out comes a single M4B with chapters and artwork.
